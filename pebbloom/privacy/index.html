Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pebbloom handles information in version 1.0 of the app.

Your task data

Pebbloom is local-first. Your tasks, reminders, life-planning data, and Pet World progress are stored on your device. Pebbloom 1.0 does not require an account and does not operate a Pebbloom backend or cloud-sync service.

Screenshot to Tasks

When you choose Screenshot to Tasks, text recognition is performed on-device using Apple system technologies. Pebbloom does not upload your screenshot to a Pebbloom server for OCR.

Import, export, widget, and sharing

You control when data leaves the main app. Export creates a file that you choose how to share or store. Import reads a file you select. The widget and share extension use the app's private shared container on the device so Pebbloom features can work together.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Pebbloom uses Apple's local notification APIs to deliver reminders you create. Notification permission is requested only when needed.

Advertising and consent

Pebbloom uses Google AdMob to display ads, including native, banner, app-open, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, consent choices, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to applicable law and your privacy choices.

Pebbloom uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required to collect and manage advertising consent choices. The app does not intentionally send the text of your tasks or OCR source images to AdMob as ad-request content.

Rewarded ads

Some optional or gated actions use rewarded ads. These include Screenshot to Tasks, optional Pet bonuses, and data import/export. The exact availability and fallback behavior depends on the feature and whether an eligible ad can be shown.

Tracking

Pebbloom does not proactively request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission unless a shipped feature requires it. Advertising behavior may vary depending on your device settings, consent choices, region, and Google Mobile Ads configuration.

Children

Pebbloom is a general-audience productivity app and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy when Pebbloom's features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to qiusuo.apps@outlook.com.
