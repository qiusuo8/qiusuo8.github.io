Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Pebbloom 1.0 stores its core app data locally on your device.

Does Pebbloom require an account?
No. Version 1.0 does not require a Pebbloom account.

Why am I seeing an ad before a feature?
Pebbloom uses advertising to support the free app. Some features use rewarded ads, and display ads may appear in eligible parts of the app.

How do I report an import or export problem?
Email support before repeatedly retrying a failing restore. Include the app version and the error message. Do not attach private backup files unless you are comfortable sharing their contents for troubleshooting.
